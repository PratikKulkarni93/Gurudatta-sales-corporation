Who we are

Gurudatta Sales Corporation is a government supplier and manufacturer of furniture.

We are dealers of a complete range of furniture for government departments, offices, institutions, hospitals, laboratories, schools, hostels and cafes. Our catalogue covers executive, workstation, conference, cafeteria and auditorium chairs, stools and bar chairs, specialty seating, sofas, dining sets and kids’ furniture, along with desks, executive, office, reception, conference, classroom and study tables, and workstations.

For storage and institutional needs, we also supply almirahs and steel storage, lockers, file cabinets and pedestals, wooden storage, compactors, hostel beds, hospital beds, examination tables and stretchers, hospital trolleys, and lab tables and chairs. Everything needed to furnish a workplace, campus or facility, from one trusted supplier.
